🛡️ Cyber Scam Awareness Center

📱 1. Social Media Security

• Never click suspicious social media links.

• Enable two-factor authentication.

• Do not share personal information with strangers.

💳 2. Digital Payment Security

• Never share your UPI PIN or OTP.

• You do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

• Verify the receiver before making payments.

💻 3. Device Security

• Keep your operating system updated.

• Use strong passwords and screen locks.

• Install apps only from trusted sources.

• Use antivirus and firewall protection.

⚖️ 4. Cyber Law in India

Information Technology Act, 2000 provides a legal framework for electronic transactions and addresses various cyber offences.

🚨 5. Famous Cyber Attacks

WannaCry: A ransomware attack that affected computers worldwide in 2017.

Pegasus: Spyware associated with surveillance of mobile devices.

Cosmos Bank Cyber Attack: A major cyber heist involving fraudulent transactions in 2018.
